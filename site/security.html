How this site is built

Clients create an account with an email and a password and stay logged in for up to 30 days. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash, never in readable form. Clients can only ever see their own requests. Our staff use a separate admin website with its own login, and the public website has no staff pages at all. Logins are rate-limited and locked after repeated wrong passwords. Password reset codes expire after 10 minutes and allow only 5 wrong tries.
