Who we are

This policy explains how [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], trading as Apeiron Solutions ("Apeiron", "we"), of [REGISTERED ADDRESS, City, Kerala, India], handles personal data collected through this website. We decide why and how that data is used, which makes us the "data fiduciary" under Indian law and the "controller" under the GDPR.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address and password. We never store your password itself: we store a one-way scrambled version (a hash made with scrypt), which cannot be turned back into your password. We also store optional company and phone details if you add them, and the date and version of the Terms and Privacy Policy you accepted.
  • Your project requests: what you want to build, the options and requirements you choose, your plan choice, the comments you and we write to each other, and the status of the request (pending, accepted or rejected) and project stage.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, pages requested and time of visit, in standard server logs at our hosting provider ([HOST]). We also keep a one-way scrambled form of your IP address to detect abuse.
  • What we do not want: payment card details, government ID numbers or other sensitive data. Please do not put them in your descriptions or comments. We do not take payments on this website.

How the form reaches us

Your details travel over an encrypted connection to our own server and are saved in our database. We send emails (for example a password-reset code, a confirmation of your request, and our replies) through our email provider ([EMAIL PROVIDER NAME]). When you send a request, our team is also emailed the details so we can review it. Email is not end-to-end encrypted unless both mail providers support it.

Why we use it

We use your details to create and run your account, keep you logged in, prepare estimates, review and answer your requests, tell you the result, and protect the site. We rely on your consent, which you give when you create an account by accepting the Terms and Conditions and this Privacy Policy, and on what is necessary to provide the service you ask for. Where the GDPR applies, our bases are consent, performing a contract with you, and our legitimate interest in running and securing the site. We do not sell your data, send marketing mailings unless you ask for them [confirm], or make automated decisions about you. The suggestions shown for your idea are made by simple keyword matching on our own server; your text is not sent to an AI service.
